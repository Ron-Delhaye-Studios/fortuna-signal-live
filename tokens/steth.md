## Definition
Lido lets users stake any amount of ETH and issues stETH, a liquid token representing a share of pooled staked ETH plus accrued rewards.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
stETH is a rebasing ERC-20 receipt: 1 ETH staked mints 1 stETH, and balances rebase daily via the AccountingOracle to reflect accrued staking rewards.

## Key risks & notes

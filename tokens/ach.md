## Definition
Alchemy Pay is a crypto-fiat payment network bridging fiat and crypto payment systems for businesses, institutions, and individuals. It provides SDKs, plugins, and APIs for online and offline payment processing.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
ACH is the ERC-20 utility token of Alchemy Pay, a crypto-fiat payment gateway founded in Singapore in 2018. Holders hold a utility token used for network transaction fees, staking rewards, merchant payment and settlement, and as a pledge that business partners lock up to access the payment network. The token was pre-mined at a 10 billion cap on September 4, 2019; it confers no ownership of the company and no claim on its revenues.

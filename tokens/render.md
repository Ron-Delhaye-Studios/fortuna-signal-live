## Definition
The Render Network is a decentralized marketplace connecting creators who need GPU rendering and AI compute with providers of idle GPU capacity.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
RENDER is the medium of exchange on the Render Network: users burn RENDER to submit GPU rendering/compute jobs, node operators earn newly minted RENDER under the burn-and-mint equilibrium model, and holders stake and vote on Render Network Proposals.

## Plain-language utility
Pays GPU providers for rendering work on the Render network.

## Key risks & notes
There is no fixed quantity of compute per token, so the compute value of RENDER floats with network pricing. The network depends on OTOY and the Render Network Foundation for stewardship and faces competition from centralized clouds and other DePIN GPU networks.

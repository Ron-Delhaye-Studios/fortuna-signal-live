## Definition
JTO is the governance token of the Jito Network, Solana's leading MEV-aware liquid staking protocol; Jito Labs also builds core Solana MEV infrastructure (a validator client run by a large share of Solana stake, a block engine, and a relayer).

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
JTO is the governance token of the Jito Network: 1 JTO = 1 vote in the Jito DAO over protocol upgrades, fee/parameter setting (JitoSOL stake pool fees, StakeNet, TipRouter), treasury management, and development. Fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 JTO. Defined in the Jito Foundation docs and Constitution.

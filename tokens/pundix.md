## Definition
Pundi X is a blockchain payments company whose XPOS terminals and wallet infrastructure let merchants accept cryptocurrency in physical retail; PUNDIX is its ecosystem utility token.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
PUNDIX is the ERC-20 utility token of the Pundi X payment ecosystem: used to pay for services within the ecosystem (XPOS point-of-sale, XWallet, XPASS) and can be delegated to Pundi X Chain validators.

## Definition
DEGEN is the community tipping and rewards token originating on Farcaster and the gas token of Degen Chain. Degen Chain is an Arbitrum Orbit layer-3 settling to Base.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
DEGEN has a dual role in the Degen ecosystem: it is the gas token of the Degen Chain Layer-3 rollup on Base used to pay transaction fees, and a community incentive and reward token used to tip content creators on Farcaster. At launch, 36,965,935,954 DEGEN were minted, allocated across airdrops, ecosystem, liquidity and team pools. Holders receive usage rights that follow the token on transfer.

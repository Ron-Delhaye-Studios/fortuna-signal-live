## Definition
Dai is the decentralized, overcollateralized stablecoin of the Maker Protocol, soft-pegged to the value of the US dollar. It is maintained by immutable Ethereum smart contracts and governed by MakerDAO token holders.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
DAI is a decentralized, fully collateralized currency soft-pegged to the value of the USD, generated by users locking crypto tokens as collateral in Maker Vaults on the Ethereum blockchain. Holder owns the token itself only; there is no issuer claim and no direct redemption channel, so value realization depends on secondary markets or vault repayment mechanics.

## Definition
Litentry is a decentralized identity aggregation protocol that links user identities across networks, letting fragmented on-chain and off-chain identity data flow across systems while preserving privacy and data sovereignty. (Note: the LIT ticker is shared with Lighter, a newer perps DEX; this entry covers Litentry, the identity protocol.)

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
LIT was the utility and governance token of Litentry, a decentralized identity-aggregation protocol on a Substrate-based network. Holders used LIT for network transaction fees, governance proposals and voting, staking to support network security, and paying for identity-related services. In January 2025 the project rebranded to Heima Network with a new HEI token; legacy LIT migrates 1:1 to HEI via an official swap portal (max supply 100 million for both). Caution: an unrelated LIT — the Lighter Infrastructure Token of a perps DEX, launched December 2025 — now shares the ticker; this dossier covers Litentry's legacy LIT only.

## Definition
Tezos is a self-amending proof-of-stake blockchain (2014, L.M. Goodman) designed so stakeholders can upgrade the protocol itself onchain without hard forks, and it supports Turing-complete smart contracts.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
Native asset of the Tezos network — pays fees, bonded by bakers as collateral, and grants on-chain governance votes. Evidence: the open protocol — the token is the chain’s native unit by consensus rules; there is no issuer to claim against.

## Plain-language utility
Fuel for the Tezos network — pays for transactions and on-chain governance.

## Key risks & notes
The 2014 whitepaper predates many amendments, so its specifics (rewards, cycles) are outdated. XTZ has staking-driven inflation that dilutes non-stakers. Competes with Ethereum L2s and newer L1s for developers and TVL, and onchain governance can concentrate among large bakers.

## Definition
Wormhole is a cross-chain interoperability protocol that lets contracts and dapps send messages and move assets between blockchains.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
W is the governance token of the Wormhole interoperability protocol. Holders stake W to delegate voting power and participate in the Wormhole DAO's multichain governance system (MultiGov), which spans Solana, Ethereum mainnet and EVM L2s. Per Wormhole's own announcement, there is no token lockup when staking for governance and unstaking is available at any time.

## Definition
Bitcoin is the first decentralized peer-to-peer electronic cash system (Satoshi Nakamoto, October 2008), enabling online payments directly between parties without a trusted financial institution.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
Native asset of the Bitcoin network — peer-to-peer currency and settlement asset; holder owns the token itself, no issuer or redemption claim. Evidence: the open protocol — the token is the chain’s native unit by consensus rules; there is no issuer to claim against.

## Plain-language utility
Peer-to-peer digital money — a bearer asset for saving and settlement, with no issuer.

## Key risks & notes
Security assumes honest nodes control a majority of hashpower; mining concentration raises 51%-attack and censorship risk. High energy use draws regulatory scrutiny. Price is volatile, and long-term risks include quantum-computing threats to cryptography and protocol ossification limiting upgrades.

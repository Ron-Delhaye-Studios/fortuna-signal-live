## Definition
Cosmos is a network of independent, parallel blockchains ('zones') connected through the Cosmos Hub, designed as an 'internet of blockchains' where assets move between chains without trusted third parties. The canonical whitepaper (Jae Kwon, Ethan Buchman) presents it as a solution to blockchain scalability, interoperability, and upgradability.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
Native asset of the Cosmos Hub — bonded to validators to secure the hub and voted in governance. Evidence: the open protocol — the token is the chain’s native unit by consensus rules; there is no issuer to claim against.

## Plain-language utility
Secures the Cosmos Hub through staking; the ecosystem's coordination token.

## Key risks & notes
ATOM's value accrual has historically been weak since thriving appchains don't require ATOM; issuance has ranged ~7-20% annually, diluting holders. Faces competition from other interoperability ecosystems and internal governance disputes over tokenomics direction.

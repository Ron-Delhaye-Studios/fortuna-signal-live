## Definition
Cryptex Finance is a US-based DeFi protocol on Ethereum building trust-minimized, on-chain financial indexes, best known for TCAP — a token that tracks the total market capitalization of the entire crypto market in real time. Its official white paper is published at cryptex.finance.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
CTX is the governance and utility token of the Cryptex protocol on Ethereum. Holders vote on-chain on protocol upgrades and treasury management, directly or by delegation; addresses with 100,000+ delegated CTX may propose executable actions, with a 3-day voting period, a 400,000-vote approval threshold, and timelock execution (2-day minimum, up to 30 days for major upgrades). Supply is fixed at 10 million CTX with no ongoing issuance and no protocol-level burn. Cryptex's TCAP and ARFI synthetic products were sunset in 2025, and the focus shifted to custody-backed index products for institutional infrastructure.

## Definition
Swell is a non-custodial Ethereum liquid staking and restaking protocol. Users stake or restake ETH and receive yield-bearing liquid tokens (swETH as an LST, rswETH as an LRT) that can be used across DeFi. (Swell publishes no standalone whitepaper PDF; its official docs are the canonical source.)

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
SWELL is the native governance and utility token of the Swell ecosystem (Ethereum liquid staking/restaking). Holders propose/vote on protocol upgrades and treasury via the Swell DAO; SWELL can be restaked (rSWELL) to help secure Swell L2 and earn rewards; it is used for gas on Swell L2. Fixed supply of 10 billion.

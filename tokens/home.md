## Definition
Defi App is a unified, non-custodial DeFi 'everything app' that aims to deliver centralized-exchange simplicity while staying decentralized, combining account abstraction across EVM and non-EVM chains with intelligent cross-chain routing for swaps, lending, and third-party dApps. Its official MiCAR whitepaper (June 2025) was published by Big Bang Studio, a Cayman Islands foundation company stewarding the protocol.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
HOME is the native token of the Defi App ecosystem: holders participate in on-chain governance of protocol upgrades, treasury allocations and parameter changes, with voting proportional to holdings at snapshot. HOME can also pay gas fees via account abstraction and be staked to unlock select platform features. The MiCA whitepaper states explicitly that the token grants no legal rights, claims or entitlements against the issuer.

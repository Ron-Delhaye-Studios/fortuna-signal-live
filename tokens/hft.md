## Definition
Hashflow is a decentralized exchange using a request-for-quote model with professional market makers to deliver zero-slippage, MEV-protected trades across chains.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
HFT is the ERC-20 governance token of Hashflow, a zero-slippage decentralized trading protocol, and of its Hashverse DAO. Holders stake and lock HFT to receive veHFT (voting-escrow tokens), with voting power weighted by the amount staked and the lock duration; veHFT holders vote on protocol fees, community treasury deployment, development roadmap, and new asset or market-maker integrations. Total supply is 1 billion HFT. The token carries no fee-switch, revenue-share, or buyback mechanism: it confers governance rights, not a claim on protocol revenue.

The RFQ model depends on professional market makers quoting honestly and competitively, introducing counterparty and centralization risk versus pure AMMs. HFT has faced heavy vesting/unlock-driven sell pressure historically.

## Definition
USDC is a United States dollar-pegged stablecoin issued by Circle, redeemable one-to-one for dollars and backed by reserves of cash and short-dated US Treasury assets held in segregated accounts.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
USDC is a redeemable claim on United States dollars at one-to-one, not an equity share or ownership title. Circle records issued stablecoins as a liability (deposits from stablecoin holders) on its balance sheet.

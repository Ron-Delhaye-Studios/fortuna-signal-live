## Definition
Space and Time is a decentralized data warehouse offering ZK-proven SQL queries: developers query indexed blockchain data with familiar SQL and receive a zero-knowledge proof (Proof of SQL) that the query and underlying data are accurate and untampered.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
SXT is the native utility token of the Space and Time verifiable data warehouse (SXT Chain): it pays for ZK-proven SQL queries and data insertions, and validators, provers, solvers and delegators must stake it to participate. Max supply is 5 billion tokens.

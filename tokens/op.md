## Definition
Optimism is the governance token of the Optimism Collective, the ecosystem behind OP Mainnet and the Superchain: a network of interoperable Ethereum Layer 2 chains built on the shared open-source OP Stack.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
OP is the governance token of the Optimism Collective (Superchain). Token House voting power follows OP holdings (directly or delegated); holders vote on protocol upgrades, token allocations, inflation adjustments, removal of the Foundation director, dissolution, elections, and ratification of governing documents. The Citizens' House separately uses a one-member-one-vote model.

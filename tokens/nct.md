## Definition
PolySwarm is a decentralized marketplace where security experts compete to detect malware and cyber threats, rewarding rapid and accurate threat intelligence.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
NCT (Nectar) is the utility token powering PolySwarm's decentralized malware-intelligence marketplace. Participants post bounties in NCT, register assertions, and establish or tear down offer channels; threat-detection engines and researchers are rewarded in NCT for accurate, timely identifications. The 2021 whitepaper extends NCT to telemetry rewards for everyday users contributing security data.

## Definition
Decentralized data lake and distributed query engine for blockchain data. Workers and Gateways provide storage, computation, and streaming of indexed chain data to applications.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
SQD is the native token of the Subsquid Network: it rewards node operators, enables permissionless selection of trustworthy operators, gates data-consumption limits for users, and is used in network governance. It confers no ownership or asset claim.

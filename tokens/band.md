## Definition
Band Protocol is a chain-agnostic decentralized data oracle: BandChain, a Cosmos-SDK blockchain, aggregates real-world data (prices and other signals) and delivers it to smart contracts on any blockchain via data tunnels.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
BAND is the native staking token of BandChain (a Cosmos SDK chain): validators and delegators stake BAND to secure block production and oracle data feeds, holders vote on protocol governance including which symbols get price feeds, and BAND is the medium of exchange on BandChain.

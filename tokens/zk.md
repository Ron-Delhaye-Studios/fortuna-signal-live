## Definition
ZKsync is an Ethereum ZK-rollup ecosystem (the Elastic Network) of interoperable ZK Chains built on the open-source ZK Stack. It uses zero-knowledge validity proofs settled to Ethereum for scaling with L1-grade security.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
ZK is the governance token of ZK Nation, governing the ZKsync protocol and its Elastic Network of ZK Stack chains (holders delegate voting power at delegate.zknation.io). It began as a pure governance instrument; a November 2025 forum proposal by Alex Gluchowski would add utility (interoperability fees, enterprise licensing revenue) but that is a proposal, not enacted token mechanics as of this research.

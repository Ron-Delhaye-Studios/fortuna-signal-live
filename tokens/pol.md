## Definition
POL is the native token of the Polygon 2.0 architecture, the designated successor to MATIC (migrated 1:1). It is designed as the coordination and growth instrument for the Polygon ecosystem and its vision of a 'Value Layer of the Internet.'

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
POL is the native gas and staking token of the Polygon 2.0 architecture and the designated successor to MATIC via a 1:1 migration. Holders hold a utility token used for gas fees, staking to secure Polygon chains (validators earn from gas, data-availability, sequencer decentralization, and more), and governance over an in-protocol Community Treasury. Initial supply is 10 billion with 2% annual emission split between validator rewards and the Community Treasury.

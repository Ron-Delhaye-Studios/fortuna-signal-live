## Definition
Aerodrome Finance is the dominant automated market maker (AMM) on the Base blockchain, using a vote-escrowed token model to direct liquidity incentives to pools.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
AERO is the protocol token of Aerodrome Finance, the DEX on Base. Holders can lock AERO as veAERO to vote on liquidity-pool reward allocations (governance) and earn trading fees and incentives; AERO is also distributed as emissions to liquidity providers.

## Plain-language utility
Governance and rewards token for the Aerodrome Finance protocol.

## Key risks & notes
Per the official whitepaper, AERO has no intrinsic value or asset backing and derives worth entirely from protocol utility. Risks disclosed: weekly emission-driven supply inflation, significant price volatility tied to Base DeFi activity, dual-token complexity (AERO vs veAERO), and regulatory uncertainty for DeFi governance tokens. Aerodrome's fortunes are concentrated on the Base chain ecosystem.

## Definition
XYO is a decentralized physical infrastructure network (DePIN) providing trustless, cryptographically verified location data to smart contracts.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
XYO is the Ethereum token powering the XYO Network: users earn it for contributing geospatial data (geomining via the COIN app and devices), and on the XYO World platform it can be traded for and staked against ERC-721 tokens representing real-world locations. It confers no ownership or asset claim.

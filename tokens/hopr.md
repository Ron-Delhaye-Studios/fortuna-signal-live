## Definition
HOPR is an open, incentivized mixnet providing metadata-private, point-to-point data exchange for Web3 — 'similar to Tor but actually private, decentralized and economically sustainable.' Node runners earn HOPR tokens on Gnosis Chain and Ethereum mainnet; it has no blockchain of its own.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
HOPR is the incentive token of the HOPR mixnet (decentralized metadata-privacy network). Node runners stake HOPR in payment channels and earn HOPR via proof-of-relay for mixing and relaying data packets; token holders also participate in the Association's DecenGov governance (quadratic voting).

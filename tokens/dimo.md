## Definition
DIMO is a vehicle-connectivity network that turns cars into user-controlled connected devices and API-accessible data sources.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
DIMO is the utility token of the DIMO connected-vehicle network with three functions per its MiCA whitepaper: governance voting on protocol decisions, network utility as the medium of exchange for on-platform actions (converted to DCX data-access credits), and incentivization rewards for drivers, node operators and developers. The token contract includes pause and upgrade roles controlled by the DIMO Foundation multisig as security safeguards.

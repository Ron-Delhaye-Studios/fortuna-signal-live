## Definition
Mantle is an Ethereum Layer-2 network and DAO-governed ecosystem, with MNT as its native gas, staking, and governance token.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
MNT is the utility and governance token of Mantle (Ethereum L2, formerly BitDAO). It pays gas fees on the Mantle network and grants governance power over Mantle DAO decisions and treasury management. It is an ERC-20 on Ethereum, also available bridge-wrapped on Mantle L2.

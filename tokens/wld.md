## Definition
Worldcoin (now branded 'World') is a digital identity and financial network aiming to establish global proof-of-personhood, so every human can prove uniqueness online and receive a share of the network's token.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
WLD is the native utility token of the Worldcoin ecosystem, intended as a payment and settlement medium within the network and as a future governance token. Its MiCA whitepaper is explicit: holders acquire no contractual rights, profit claims, ownership interests, or enforceable obligations against any legal entity.

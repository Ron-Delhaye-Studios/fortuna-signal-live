## Definition
Cartesi is a rollup and computation platform that brings a full Linux environment to blockchain dApps, letting developers use familiar languages and tools while settling verifiably on-chain.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
CTSI is the utility token of Cartesi (app-specific rollups with a Linux VM). It is staked in the Proof-of-Stake consensus system (directly, delegated to pools, or via custodians), pays fees for the Noether data-availability sidechain, and holders participate in community governance.

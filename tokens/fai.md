## Definition
Freysa is a 'sovereign AI agent' experiment that began as a gamified prompt-injection challenge (players paid escalating fees to try to convince the AI to release a prize pool) and is evolving into infrastructure for autonomous, verifiable AI agents.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
The issuer's MiCAR white paper describes FAI as the access and governance token of the Freysa AI game and agent ecosystem: holders get access to experiences, priority in Freysa's decision-making conversations, and developers would use FAI to pay for SDK and agent-deployment infrastructure. Coinbase corroborates the game-access and agent-deployment utility. However, several of the described functions (governance participation, SDK payments, compute co-ownership) are framed as planned or long-term, not active, so the live utility is only partially confirmed.

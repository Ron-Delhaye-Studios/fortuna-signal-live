## Definition
EVM-compatible blockchain built with the Cosmos SDK, interoperating via IBC, positioned as the chain powering the Crypto.com ecosystem.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
CRO is the native asset of the Crypto.org Chain (Cronos POS chain) and of the Cronos EVM chain. It is staked by validators for consensus, paid as staking rewards, spent as transaction/gas fees, and used for on-chain governance. It also exists as an ERC-20 on Ethereum, bridged to native CRO.

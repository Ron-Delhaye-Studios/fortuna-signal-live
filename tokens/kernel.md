## Definition
KernelDAO is a restaking protocol operating across BNB Chain and Ethereum with three product lines: Kernel (core restaking infrastructure for BTC, BNB, and reward-bearing tokens), Kelp (a liquid restaking token protocol on Ethereum), and Gain (tokenized reward-farming vaults across crypto and RWAs).

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
KERNEL is the unified governance token across the KernelDAO ecosystem (Kelp LRT, Kernel restaking, Gain vaults): holders stake KERNEL to vote on protocol parameters and upgrades, and can provide liquidity; also used to secure restaked services for rewards. Total/max supply 1,000,000,000.

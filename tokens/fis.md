## Definition
StaFi is a cross-chain liquid staking protocol issuing rTokens that represent staked principal plus staking rewards. It lets users keep liquidity while their assets are staked.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
FIS is the native token of the StaFi chain (a Substrate-based chain using NPoS consensus), a liquid-staking protocol. Holders use FIS to pay network transaction fees, participate in governance voting, stake as validators or nominators to secure the chain and earn rewards, and provide liquidity. Total supply is 800 million FIS. Note the separate rTokens (rETH, rDOT, rKSM, etc.) are the receipts representing users' staked assets — FIS itself is not a claim on those assets.

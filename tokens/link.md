## Definition
Chainlink is the dominant decentralized oracle network connecting blockchains to offchain data, computation, and cross-chain messaging.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
LINK is the bearer utility asset of the Chainlink Network: developers pay fees in LINK to access oracle services (data feeds, VRF, CCIP, automation), and node operators receive LINK as compensation and stake it as cryptoeconomic collateral subject to slashing. It confers no ownership or revenue rights.

## Key risks & notes
Chainlink Labs and the team retain a large share of LINK (team/reserves/ecosystem allocations), and node-operator economics face fee pressure as oracle services commoditize. Competition from alternative oracle providers is ongoing.

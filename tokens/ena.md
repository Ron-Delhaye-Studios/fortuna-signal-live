## Definition
Ethena is a crypto-native dollar protocol issuing USDe, a synthetic dollar backed by crypto assets and corresponding short futures positions, plus the staked version sUSDe.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
ENA is the governance token of the Ethena protocol: holders vote on key protocol decisions and elect Risk Committee members biannually; ENA can be locked to sENA for voting and rewards.

## Definition
DAR is the native token of Mines of Dalarnia, a browser-based action-adventure mining game by Chromia Studios and Workinman Interactive with a blockchain real-estate market, now evolving into the broader DAR Open Network.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
Holder owns DAR, the native token of Mines of Dalarnia and the DAR Open Network. Sources describing the official announcement and litepaper document its roles: default in-game currency for upgrades, fees, and NFT marketplace trades, staking for progression, and governance over miner policies and the land economy. Note: DAR is migrating 1:1 to the new D token via an official portal open until December 20, 2025, so a holder's claim is effectively on the migrated token.

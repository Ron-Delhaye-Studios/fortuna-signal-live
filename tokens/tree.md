## Definition
TREE is the governance and data token of Treehouse, a decentralized fixed-income infrastructure protocol that produces Decentralized Offered Rates (DOR) - on-chain interest-rate benchmarks for lending rates, staking rates, and forward-rate products.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
TREE is the ERC-20 governance/staking token of Treehouse's fixed-income infrastructure: staked to support DOR panelists via Pre-Deposit Vaults, used for governance and protocol rewards, and paid as on-chain fees for benchmark rate data. Total supply is 1 billion.

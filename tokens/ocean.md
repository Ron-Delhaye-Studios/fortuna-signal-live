## Definition
Ocean Protocol is a decentralized protocol and network for AI data and services, aiming to spread the benefits of AI by unlocking data for sale while preserving privacy. It powers data marketplaces, data commons tooling, and AI/data-science consumption.

## What the holder owns (FORA-01 Q1)
OCEAN is Ocean Protocol's utility token for its decentralized data marketplace: holders lock OCEAN for veOCEAN, stake on datasets to earn a share of data-consumption revenue, and use OCEAN as the medium of value transfer in the data economy. It confers no ownership or asset claim.
